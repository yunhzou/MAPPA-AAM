Exact boundary-profile reuse: local Golden comparison

Nonzero bond-weight rows are cached once per immutable target. Filtering them by the candidate outside set produces the same ordered boundary constraints as a full matrix scan. Sub-threshold nonzero weights are retained. Matching, compression, growth, seeds, caps and scoring are unchanged.

Three cases, 3 fresh-process repetitions per direction per revision, one process at a time; median sum of the two directional process CPU times. Search, symmetry finalization and checkpoint output included; imports, decoding and graph hashing excluded. Same corrected algorithm, inputs, seeds and caps; only the boundary-profile cache differs.

All 18 comparisons have identical full search-graph SHA-256 hashes, covering contexts, states, transitions, stops and finalized symmetry records. All 116 targeted tests passed, including direct full-matrix equality, independent injective enumeration and native/standard finalizer equality.

The gains are modest and specific to these three cases and repetitions. This is not a full-dataset speedup estimate or a comparison isolating differences from the historical paper implementation. The baseline is the corrected published commit 4445342.
